Workstation Inventory & Automation

A practical IT workflow for collecting workstation information, standardizing asset records and reducing repetitive inventory work.

PowerShellWindowsInventoryIntune workflowsAutomation

Problem

Workstation information was distributed across users and machines, making inventory completion, naming and follow-up difficult to manage consistently.

Objective

Create a repeatable way to collect key device information and organize it into a central asset workflow.

What I worked on

  • Collection of computer name, manufacturer, model, serial number, MAC address and user information.
  • PowerShell scripting to reduce manual collection steps.
  • Structured tracking of completed and outstanding workstations.
  • Workstation naming and rollout organization.
  • Exploration of centralized deployment through Microsoft Intune where applicable.

Engineering lesson

A useful automation project is not only about writing a script. The bigger value comes from designing a repeatable process around the script: consistent data, clear status, predictable naming and fewer manual steps.
